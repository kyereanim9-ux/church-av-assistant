# One More Morning

**Idea:** A man whose heart was broken chooses, one small morning habit at a
time, to become who he wants to be.

## Story (15 seconds, no dialogue)

1. **Before dawn.** Blue, cold bedroom. Elias lies awake on one side of a bed
   made for two. On the nightstand, a framed photo lies face down.
2. **The first choice.** His bare feet touch the floor. He pulls the curtain
   open; a thin line of gold light crosses the room.
3. **Small habits.** He makes the bed. He laces worn running shoes. He writes
   one line in a small notebook beside a steaming cup.
4. **The door.** He opens the front door and runs into the sunrise, small in
   a wide golden street. He breathes out, and almost smiles.

The change is shown by the light: the film starts cold blue and ends warm gold.
