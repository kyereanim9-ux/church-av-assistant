# Last Ferry

**Idea:** A courier on a bicycle has to reach the ferry before the ramp lifts.

## Story (15 seconds, no dialogue)

1. **The horn.** A ferry horn sounds over a harbour town. Mara, mid-pedal at
   the top of a steep street, looks up: far below, the ferry is still at the
   dock, its ramp down.
2. **The descent.** She dives down the hill, weaving past market crates and a
   startled gull, the orange bag bouncing on her back.
3. **The ramp moves.** On the quay, the ramp begins to rise. A gap of dark
   water opens between ramp and dock.
4. **The jump.** She stands on the pedals, hits the edge of the rising ramp
   and lands on the deck, skidding to a stop.
5. **Made it.** She laughs, out of breath, as the ferry pulls away into
   golden water.

The change is shown by distance and light: the ferry starts tiny and far below
in cool shade, and ends filling the frame in warm gold.
