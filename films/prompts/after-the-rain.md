# After the Rain

**Idea:** A man who got his heart broken.

## Story (15 seconds, no dialogue)

1. **Stood up.** Night, heavy rain. Elias waits alone on an old stone bridge
   under a streetlamp, holding a small bouquet of red tulips. His phone
   lights his face, then goes dark.
2. **It lands.** His hand with the bouquet drops to his side. Rain runs down
   his face; you cannot tell rain from tears.
3. **Letting go.** He rests the tulips on the wet stone railing, looks at
   them for a moment, and leaves them there.
4. **Walking on.** He walks away across the bridge. Behind him the rain thins
   and stops.
5. **First light.** At the far end he pauses and lifts his face. A line of
   rose-gold dawn opens under the clouds and reaches the tulips on the
   railing.

The change is shown by the weather and the light: heavy rain in cold navy at
the start, still air and warm dawn at the end.
